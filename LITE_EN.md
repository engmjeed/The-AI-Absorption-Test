# The AI Absorption Test — Lite v0.2

Use this for a fast first-pass discussion. Do not produce a 100-point score.

Evaluate the product with only three questions:

1. **10× AI:** If models become 10× smarter, faster, and cheaper, what part of the product is still necessary?
2. **Compounding core:** What data, state, workflow, trust, distribution, relationship, permission, or real-world asset grows with use and cannot be copied quickly?
3. **100× AI adoption:** If AI usage grows 100×, does the problem this product solves become larger or smaller?

Then return:
- **Likely absorption risk:** low / medium / high
- **Absorbable shell:** what AI is likely to commoditize
- **Defensible direction:** what should become the core
- **One survival move:** the highest-leverage redesign
- **Decisive question:** one question that could invalidate the idea

Be critical. Do not treat AI usage, UI, prompts, or generic automation as a moat.
